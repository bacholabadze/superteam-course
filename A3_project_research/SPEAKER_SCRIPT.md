# 1-minute talk: Phantom

Hard limit is 60 seconds. The script is ~110 spoken words (~50 s at a calm pace), which leaves a 10 s buffer.

| Time | Slide | Point |
|---|---|---|
| 0–10 s | 1 | What Phantom is, who uses it, ~17M MAU |
| 10–25 s | 1 | Workflow + why Solana |
| 25–35 s | 1 | Business model: 0.85% swap fee |
| 35–50 s | 2 | On-chain vs off-chain: standard programs, simulation |
| 50–60 s | 2 | Risks & improvement → takeaway |

## Delivery tips
- Don't read the slides aloud. Point at the **17M** and **0.85%** numbers on slide 1, then switch to slide 2 at "technically…".
- If you run long, cut the "Solana აქ იმიტომაა…" sentence first. The slide already covers it.
- Rehearse once with a timer. Georgian speech runs longer than the word count suggests.
